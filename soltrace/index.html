Follow the sun. See the shadows.

Solar position, sun path in augmented reality and shadow simulation, with a calculation engine that works offline.

Not yet available on the App Store
iPhone, iPad

NREL solar position

Solar position from the NREL Solar Position Algorithm, with sunrise, sunset and polar-day events and daylight-saving handling.

AR Sun

Overlay the calculated path of the sun on the camera image.

AR Shadows

Simulate shadows manually, in AR without LiDAR, or in AR with LiDAR on devices that have it.

Map and charts

Sun trajectories on a map and charts of solar angles through the day and the year.

Projects and reports

Save sites as projects and export PDF reports and CSV files.

Please note. AR alignment depends on your device’s sensors and on heading calibration. Treat AR sun paths and shadows as indicative.

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later. AR modes need a physical device; LiDAR modes need a device with LiDAR. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
