Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

SolTrace does not collect, sell or share personal data, and the developer receives none of your data. A few optional features contact public services directly (weather, elevation, address search); see “Network requests” below. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. If you use the iCloud features, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your projects and sites: names, coordinates, dates, settings and the results you choose to save.
  • Your settings: language and appearance.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: used in the AR Sun and AR Shadows modes to overlay the calculated sun position and simulated shadows. Camera frames stay on your device.
  • Location (while using the app): used only when you ask for it, to set the site coordinates and to calibrate the true-north heading. See “Network requests” for what can be sent.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

Network requests

Some features contact online services directly. Automatic weather (Settings) uses Apple Weather (WeatherKit): the coordinates of the selected site and the time are sent to Apple, and Apple’s terms apply (attribution: https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html). The ground elevation of a chosen place and the terrain heights used for the horizon are requested from Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): latitude and longitude are sent. When you download the relief, about 1,150 points around the place (up to 50 km) are sent as well. Open-Meteo states that its server logs, which may contain coordinates, are deleted after 90 days. Address search uses Apple Maps first; only when you validate a search or tap the button, OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) may be used, and the text you typed and your language are sent. These services necessarily receive your IP address and follow their own privacy policies. Open-Meteo states that its server logs, which may contain coordinates, are deleted after 90 days. Nothing is sent to us.

iCloud

iCloud sync is on by default and optional: you can turn it off in Settings. Your sites and projects are synced through your private iCloud database, and your preferences (language, units, coordinate format, appearance, time zone, altitude and weather options) are backed up in iCloud key-value storage so that they follow you to a new device.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Sharing

SolTrace sends nothing to us. The only requests to third parties are those listed in “Network requests”. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

SolTrace is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
