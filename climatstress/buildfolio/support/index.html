Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does Buildfolio collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Does Buildfolio change my ZIP archives?

No. Archives are imported in read-only mode and the app keeps its own copy and SHA-256 snapshot.

Where are my files stored?

In a visible Buildfolio folder in your iCloud Drive and in a local copy on the device. See the privacy policy for the details.
