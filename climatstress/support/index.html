Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does ClimatStress collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is the core-temperature figure a measurement?

No. It is a model-based estimate from heart rate. Always follow your site’s HSE procedures and see a doctor for health concerns.

Does the app write my health data to iCloud?

No. When the iPhone is out of reach, the watch relays only the state of the session (work, break, deadlines, alert identifiers), never heart rate or temperature estimates.

Why does the app save a workout in Apple Health?

To keep the work session running in the background on the watch.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also restore purchases from the app’s settings.
