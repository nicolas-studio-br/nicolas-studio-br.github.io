Heat-stress decision support for construction sites.

Heat Index or WBGT, work/rest cycles by flag and Apple Watch alerts before each break. A sentinel-type aid: it alerts and helps you think about heat, nothing more.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, Apple Watch

Heat Index and WBGT

Heat Index (NWS Rothfusz) or WBGT, measured, direct or estimated with the Liljegren et al. (2008) model, with editable thresholds.

Work/rest cycles

Cycles by flag, from green to black, given as maxima.

Apple Watch alerts

Haptic and sound alert 5 minutes before the break, and a green light to resume.

Core-temperature estimate

Estimated from heart rate. It is a model, not a measurement.

Daily HSE report

A daily report in CSV and PDF, saved in the ClimatStress folder of the Files app.

Please note. ClimatStress is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. It does not replace medical advice, the regulations in force, your employer’s HSE recommendations or your own judgement. If you feel unwell, stop work and call the emergency services, whatever the app shows.

Pricing

All features are free for 3 days; then the Heat Index and WBGT calculators stay free. Monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual subscriptions, or a lifetime purchase, unlock the rest. Payments go through Apple.

App name. On this website the app is called ClimatStress. In the versions distributed outside the United States, the app shows the name “Calorvium: Heat Stress” under its icon and on screen.

Requires iPhone with iOS 18 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
