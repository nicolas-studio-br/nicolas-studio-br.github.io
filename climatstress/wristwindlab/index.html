An experiment: estimating wind with your wrist.

An experimental fusion of Apple Watch sensors (motion, barometer, microphone, weather) to estimate wind speed. It is not an anemometer.

Not yet available on the App Store
Apple Watch, iPhone

Sensor fusion

Combines the motion unit, barometer, microphone noise and weather data on the watch.

Calibration

Measure the floor in calm air, then record points against a reference anemometer.

Mean speed and gusts

Mean speed over an adjustable window, 3-second gust and an internal uncertainty.

Session journal

A CSV journal of derived values only, no audio, transferred to your iPhone.

iPhone companion

A guide, the watch status and the limits, on your iPhone.

Please note. WristWind Lab is experimental and is not an anemometer. No accuracy is guaranteed before a field test against a reference anemometer. Never use it for safety decisions (HSE, lifting, weather).

Requires Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later and an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. A physical watch is recommended; an Apple Watch Ultra gives access to dual-frequency GNSS. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português.
