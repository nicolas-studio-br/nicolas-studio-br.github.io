Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does SetVision Protocol collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

How reliable are the measurements?

They depend on a steady support, good light and a clear view of the joint. The app flags sets with weak tracking, no scale, an imported video, few repetitions or a reduced analysis rate. See the Method page.

Does the app read my Health data?

No. It can only write a workout, if you allow it.

Where do my sets and videos go?

They stay on your device and, if iCloud is on, in the app’s iCloud Drive container in your own account. The developer has no access.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also restore purchases from the app’s settings.
