Method – SetVision Protocol

How SetVision turns a video into numbers (analysis version 2). All constants are fixed in the app’s analysis code.

1. Signal

For each frame, Apple’s Vision framework returns the normalised position of the tracked joint; the side detected with more confidence is kept. Vertical position in pixels: y = y_norm × H. Samples with confidence below 0.3 are discarded, gaps of 0.25 s or less are linearly interpolated, and the signal is resampled at the video frame rate fs. Smoothing is a zero-phase Gaussian with σ = 0.06 s; velocity is the central difference vi = (si+1 − si−1) · fs / 2.

2. Repetitions

Turning points are found with a hysteresis h = max(0.35 × (P95 − P5), ROMmin), with ROMmin = 0.08 m (calibrated) or 25 px. The concentric window runs between a minimum and the next maximum, using a velocity threshold vth = 0.05 × vpeak; both ends must be at rest, otherwise the repetition is partial and rejected. The mean concentric velocity is MCV = Δy / Δt over that window. A repetition is rejected if its range of motion is under 50 % of the median, its duration is outside 0.15–8 s, or fewer than 80 % of its frames are tracked.

3. Set statistics

Velocity loss VL = (vbest − vlast) / vbest × 100 (Sánchez-Medina & González-Badillo, 2011). CV = SD / mean × 100. The linear slope of MCV over the repetition index and its R² are also given. Method sensitivity is the SD of MCV over four perturbed settings.

4. Scale

m/px = Lref / dpx, measured on a frame of height H₀. For a video of height H: m/pxvideo = m/px × H₀ / H, assuming the same field of view.

5. Trend

Best-repetition MCV of sets at the same load (±0.05 kg). Baseline = first 3 sets, recent = mean of the last 3 or fewer. Smallest detectable change SDC = 1.96 × √2 × noise ≈ 2.77 × noise (Weir, 2005), where noise is the larger of the baseline CV and the mean method sensitivity.

6. Load–velocity profile

From the most recent calibrated set at each load within 21 days, with at least 3 loads: least squares v = a + b · load; LD0 = −a / b; estimated 1RM = (v1RM − a) / b (Jidovtseff et al., 2011). Shown as an estimate only (Banyard et al., 2017; Hughes et al., 2019).

7. Quality flags

A set is flagged when fewer than 90 % of frames are tracked, when there is no scale, when the video was imported, when it has fewer than 3 repetitions, or when it was analysed at under 25 frames per second (for example under thermal pressure, when the app analyses fewer frames). Flags appear with the result and in the PDF report.

8. Shared protocol files

A .svprotocol file (JSON) holds the exercise name, joint, direction, orientation, frame rate, velocity-loss target, 1RM velocity, framing note, export date and a SHA-256 checksum. The pixel scale and the reference image are never exported. The checksum detects corruption or hand edits; it is not a signature.

9. Known limits

MCV is defined from a 5 % velocity threshold, so it is not identical to the definition of a linear position transducer, but it is consistent from set to set. Vision tracks a joint, not the bar. No validation against a reference device has been carried out for this app. Slow-motion videos imported from Photos may carry a retimed timeline, which is not detected.
