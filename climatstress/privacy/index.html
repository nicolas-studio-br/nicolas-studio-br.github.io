Privacy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary

ClimatStress has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer receives none of your data. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Local weather is requested from Apple Weather when you ask for it (see below).

What is stored on your device

  • Data you enter: air temperature, humidity, wind, solar radiation, Heat Index or WBGT values, and your settings.
  • Reports (CSV, PDF), saved in the ClimatStress folder of the Files app. All data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • The date of first launch is stored in the device Keychain to compute the 3-day free period. It is not sent anywhere.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Apple Health (read, with your permission): age, sex, resting heart rate, body mass, height, body fat, VO2max and live heart rate from your Apple Watch, to fill the model parameters, and the body-temperature readings of the previous 90 days (up to 500), used on your device to check the latest reading. You can always type your own values.
  • Apple Health (write): the work session is saved as a workout, to keep it running in the background.
  • Location (while using the app): to fetch local weather and to print the site coordinates in your report. The position is never sent to the developer.
  • Motion and barometer: to detect whether you are still, to recognise a break, and to compare atmospheric pressure with Apple Weather.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.
  • Notifications: local alerts and reminders created on your device. No push service operated by us is involved.

iCloud

ClimatStress does not write health data to iCloud. When the iPhone is out of reach and the watch has Wi-Fi or cellular access, the watch relays only the state of the session (work, break, deadlines, alert identifiers; never the heart rate or the temperature estimate) through your private iCloud database until the iPhone has read it; the records are then deleted and nothing is sent to the developer. If you copy a report to iCloud Drive yourself, that copy is governed by your iCloud settings.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Network requests

When you tap the weather button, your approximate location is sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to obtain local weather. Apple’s own privacy terms apply to that request. Apple Weather data attribution: https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your purchase is active.

Sharing

ClimatStress shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

ClimatStress is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
