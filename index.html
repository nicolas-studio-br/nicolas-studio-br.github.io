Apps for the field, the gym and the sky.

Nicolas_Studio designs independent apps for Apple devices. Each app has its own page, privacy policy, terms and support.

SiteNotes

Write down what you see on site: photos, position, status and who owns each action.

Talistag

Locks distracting apps and unlocks them only with your NFC tags.

AeroTrace

An experimental research tool: record or import the sound of an aircraft, analyse its Doppler shift and compare it with public flight data.

SetVision Protocol

Film a set on a tripod. SetVision tracks one joint, measures the mean concentric velocity of each repetition, the velocity loss of the set and your trend from session to session.

Buildfolio

Import your projects as ZIP archives in read-only mode, keep SHA-256 snapshots, track versions and builds, and write notes for each revision.

SolTrace

Solar position, sun path in augmented reality and shadow simulation, with a calculation engine that works offline.

FieldDatum

Survey QA, coordinate calculations and indicative AR measurements for site engineers, with documented observations.

RoadStrata

Build a pavement section layer by layer, estimate traffic, explore thickness scenarios and produce reports, as a preliminary assessment.
