Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

AeroTrace has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and the developer operates no server. Your recordings stay on your device. When you use the online flight search, the app contacts public flight-data services directly (see “Network requests” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your observations: country, time zone, date and time range, notes, the name of the analysed file and analysis results. They are saved as a private file inside the app. The audio or video file itself is not kept.
  • Recordings made on Apple Watch are transferred to your iPhone.
  • If you enter OpenSky Network credentials, they are kept in the system Keychain.
  • Your settings and your purchase status, provided by Apple.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Microphone: to record ambient sound so the app can analyse the Doppler shift of aircraft, on iPhone and Apple Watch. Recordings are analysed on your device.
  • Location (while using the app): attached to a recording only when you turn that option on.
  • Files and photos: you choose the audio or video files to analyse. They stay where they are; the app does not keep a copy of the file.
  • Apple Intelligence: when available, the app can read details (country, time zone, date, time) from the free text of your notes with Apple’s on-device model (Foundation Models). The text stays on your device and every value is checked by the app.

Network requests

When you use the online flight search, AeroTrace connects directly to public flight-data services. To adsb.lol it sends the search area: a latitude and longitude rounded to four decimals and a radius. To the OpenSky Network, only if you entered your own credentials, it sends those credentials to OpenSky’s sign-in service and then requests aircraft states. These services necessarily receive your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies. The app uses ephemeral network sessions and does not keep cookies or a cache. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your purchase is active.

Sharing

AeroTrace shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

AeroTrace is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
