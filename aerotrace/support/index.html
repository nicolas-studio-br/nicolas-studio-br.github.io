Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does AeroTrace collect my data?

Not by us: the developer operates no server. The only network requests are the optional online flight searches described in the privacy policy.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Audio is analysed on your device. Only the search area (a latitude and longitude rounded to four decimals, and a radius) is sent to the flight-data service when you use the online search.

Do I need an OpenSky Network account?

No. It is optional. If you enter your own credentials, they are stored in the system Keychain and sent only to OpenSky’s sign-in service.

How accurate is the analysis?

It is exploratory. Digital levels are not calibrated sound pressure, and only the first audio track is analysed.
