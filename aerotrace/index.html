Listen to the sky. Compare it with the data.

An experimental research tool: record or import the sound of an aircraft, analyse its Doppler shift and compare it with public flight data.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch

Import or record

Choose audio or video files (MOV, MP4, M4A, WAV) or record on iPhone or Apple Watch.

Doppler analysis

Spectral analysis of up to 30 seconds of audio and a Doppler forward model to compare with candidate trajectories.

Public flight data

Search aircraft around a place with adsb.lol or, with your own credentials, the OpenSky Network.

Local history

Observations are stored on your device. The app does not keep a copy of the files you analyse.

Apple Intelligence (optional)

Where available, on-device Apple Intelligence can read dates and places from your notes. Every value is checked in code.

Please note. AeroTrace is experimental. Its results are estimates for research: they are not proof of a recording’s location or of an aircraft’s identity.

Pricing

AeroTrace offers a 3-day access period and a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments go through Apple.

Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Apple Watch is used for recording. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
