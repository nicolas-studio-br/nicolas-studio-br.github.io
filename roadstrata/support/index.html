Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does RoadStrata collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Can I use the results to design a pavement?

No. Results are preliminary. A pavement must be designed with a validated method by a qualified engineer.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The developer has no access.

Why does the app ask for notifications?

iCloud uses silent background notifications to tell your devices that data has changed. They carry no content from your projects.
