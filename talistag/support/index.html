Support

Need help? Start here, or write to .All apps · nicolas_studio@proton.me

How do I write a tag?

Open Groups, choose or create a group, then tap “Write a tag” and hold your iPhone near an empty NFC tag. A master tag unlocks every group.

Which tags work?

Writable NFC Forum tags such as NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 (stickers, cards or keyrings). Your iPhone must have NFC (iPhone 7 or later).

I lost my tag. What now?

Apps unlock by themselves when the schedule ends, when you arrive in a safe zone or when you join a known Wi-Fi. As a last resort, deleting Talistag removes all blocking. Keep a spare master tag somewhere safe.

Why can't I edit groups or rules?

While a group is locked, settings are frozen so they cannot be used to bypass the lock. Scan a tag first.

Safe zones do not work reliably.

Set Location to “Always” with Precise Location on, keep Background App Refresh enabled and use a radius of 200 m or more.

Wi-Fi is not detected.

Reading the network name needs Location access. Add the network while connected to it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also use “Restore purchases” in Talistag › Settings.

Does Talistag collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
