What Talistag does

Talistag uses Apple's Screen Time technology to shield apps and websites you choose, and lets NFC tags you wrote unlock them for a limited time. It is a self-control tool, not a security product.

Limits you should know

  • Deleting the app or revoking Screen Time access removes the blocking.
  • If you lose your tags, apps stay locked while a rule applies. Unlocking happens when the schedule ends, when you reach a safe zone or a known Wi-Fi, or by removing the app. Keep a spare master tag.
  • Do not rely on Talistag for safety-critical or emergency needs. Apps you do not select, such as Phone, are never blocked.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple.
