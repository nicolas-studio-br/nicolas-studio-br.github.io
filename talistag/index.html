Your apps stay locked.
A tag is the key.

Talistag locks the apps that distract or expose you — banks, mail, social — and only your own NFC tags unlock them. Rules decide when: places, schedules, Wi-Fi.

Not yet available on the App Store

Tap to unlock

Hold your iPhone near a tag you wrote yourself to unlock a group of apps for 15 minutes up to 2 hours.

Safe zones

At home or at the office, apps never lock. Step outside and they do.

Schedules and Wi-Fi

Lock only at night or on weekdays, and trust your home Wi-Fi.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no server. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Four languages

English, Français, Español, Português — switch inside the app, instantly.

Built on Apple Screen Time

Uses Apple's own Screen Time technology for reliable blocking.

How it works

  1. Choose the apps and sites to protect.
  2. Write a tag and keep it out of reach.
  3. Set your rules: safe zones, schedule, known Wi-Fi.
  4. Scan the tag when you really need access.

Pricing

Try it free for 3 days, then choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments go through Apple; manage or cancel any time in your Apple account settings.

Talistag works on iPhone with iOS 18 or later. NFC tags are sold separately.
