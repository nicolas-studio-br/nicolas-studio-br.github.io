Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Talistag does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Everything the app knows stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • The apps and websites you choose to block. Apple's Screen Time framework gives Talistag opaque tokens: Talistag cannot read their names and cannot see how you use your apps.
  • Your groups, tags (a random identifier and a name), schedule, unlock duration and chosen language.
  • Safe zones (the coordinates and radius you saved) and the names of Wi-Fi networks you add.
  • Face ID: used through iOS to confirm it is you when needed. It is handled entirely by iOS; Talistag never receives your biometric data.
  • Your purchase status, provided by Apple.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Screen Time (Family Controls): to apply and remove the blocking.
  • NFC: to write and read your tags.
  • Location (“Always” recommended): to detect when you enter or leave a safe zone. Location is processed on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • Wi-Fi network information: to compare the current network name with the networks you saved.

Sharing

Talistag shares nothing with us or with third parties. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details.

NFC tags

A tag contains only a web address with a random identifier. It holds no personal information. If another phone scans your tag, it opens a generic page on this website.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete items in the app or delete the app, which removes all its data and ends all blocking. Device backups made by iOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

Talistag is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
