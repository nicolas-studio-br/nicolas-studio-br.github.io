Measure your bar speed, set after set.

Film a set on a tripod. SetVision tracks one joint, measures the mean concentric velocity of each repetition, the velocity loss of the set and your trend from session to session.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, Apple Watch

Analysis on your device

Apple’s Vision framework tracks the joint frame by frame. Your videos are not uploaded.

Velocity loss

See how much speed you lose across a set and when a velocity-loss target is reached. The calculation is documented on the Method page.

Trends and load–velocity profile

Compare sessions at the same load with a smallest detectable change, and estimate a load–velocity profile. Estimates are shown as estimates only.

iPhone and Apple Watch

A companion app for Apple Watch and a widget for your Home Screen.

Reports and sharing

Create PDF reports and share protocol files with a checksum. The pixel scale and reference image are never exported.

Apple Health (write only)

Optionally save each analysed set as a strength-training workout. Nothing is read from Health.

Please note. SetVision measures movement from video. It is a training aid, not a medical device, and results depend on framing, light and a steady support.

Pricing

Some features, such as export, are part of SetVision Pro, available as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments go through Apple; manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

Requires iPhone; Apple Watch is optional. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
