Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

SetVision Protocol does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. If you use the iCloud features, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your sets and analysis results: repetitions, velocities, quality flags, load, exercise name and notes.
  • The videos and the scale or reference image you use for calibration. Protocol files you export never include the pixel scale or the reference image.
  • Your settings and your purchase status, provided by Apple.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: to film your set. The video is analysed on your device with Apple’s Vision framework.
  • Photos: you choose the photos to import with Apple’s photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select, not your whole library.
  • Motion sensors: used on the device to check that the iPhone is steady before a set. This data is not stored or sent.
  • Health (write only): the app can save each analysed set to Apple Health as a strength-training workout. It does not read any Health data.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

iCloud

SetVision can store its data in the app’s iCloud Drive container, so your sets are available on your devices.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your purchase is active.

Sharing

SetVision Protocol shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

SetVision Protocol is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
