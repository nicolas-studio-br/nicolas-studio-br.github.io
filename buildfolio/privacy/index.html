Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Buildfolio does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. If you use the iCloud features, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • The ZIP archives you import, their SHA-256 snapshots, versions, builds, revision notes and roadmap.
  • Your settings: language and appearance.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Files: you choose which files the app opens. The app reads only the files you select.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

iCloud

Buildfolio stores its files in a visible Buildfolio folder in your iCloud Drive, with a local copy on the device.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Sharing

Buildfolio shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

Buildfolio is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
