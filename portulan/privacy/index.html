Privacy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary

Portulan does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and the app’s privacy manifest declares no data collection and no tracking. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. The app contacts the internet only when you ask it to, for the sources and services described below.

What is stored on your device

  • Your investigations: images, documents, citations, notes, dates, conclusions, maps, source captures and editions. Files are kept in a local vault, identified by their SHA-256 fingerprint, and working copies are kept in the app’s storage.
  • Your settings, such as appearance and guided view.
  • An optional local test journal (actions and times, 1,000 entries at most). It stays on the device and is shared only if you choose to export it.
  • A local record of the network requests made by the Pro workshop (host and purpose), shown to you in the app.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: to take a field photograph. Location (while using the app) and Motion: to record the position, its accuracy and the camera tilt at the time of capture.
  • Face ID or passcode: to open protected investigations.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe a selected clip. The app uses it only when the language and the device support on-device recognition.
  • Notifications: for source-watch results, and on the Apple Watch for the alerts you allow. They are generated on your devices.
  • Background refresh: source checks run opportunistically, when the system grants time.
  • Files: you choose which files to open or where to export; on Mac the app reads only what you select.
  • Apple Intelligence and Translation: summaries use Apple’s on-device model when available; translations use Apple’s Translation framework. No AI service operated by us is involved.
  • Safari: the share extension passes the page or the selected image to the app through a shared container (App Group) on your device.

Network requests

  • Sources you add or open (a page, an image, a data file, a map download): the app requests that address directly, over HTTPS, and refuses addresses that are not public. The site you contact sees the request like any browser visit, including your IP address.
  • Pro connectors: only to the public services listed in the app (web.archive.org, api.gleif.org, recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr, brasilapi.com.br, archive-api.open-meteo.com, overpass-api.de, www.bodacc.fr and crt.sh), when you launch a query.
  • Timestamping: when you request an RFC 3161 timestamp, the app sends the SHA-256 fingerprint of the item and a random number to freetsa.org. The item itself is not sent.
  • Search engines and archive gateways open in your browser; their own privacy policies apply.

iCloud

Portulan uses your own iCloud Drive (an app container) to exchange investigations between your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. If iCloud Drive is unavailable, working copies stay on the device. The developer cannot see your data and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Apple Watch

The watch receives from the paired iPhone up to twenty recent sheets, never the full photographs, and keeps the last one locally. A note dictated on the watch is handled by the system dictation service.

Sharing and exports

Portulan shares nothing with us or with third parties. The PDFs, ZIP files and .portulan files you export may reveal places, names and metadata; photographs in a ZIP are re-encoded without their original metadata by default, but check the content and the licences before you share. Exports can be encrypted with a passphrase that you choose; we cannot recover it.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Data synced with iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings. Backups made by your system may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

Portulan is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
