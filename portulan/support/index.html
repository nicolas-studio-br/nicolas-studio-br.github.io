Support

Need help or have a suggestion? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does Portulan collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details, including the few network requests the app makes when you ask it to.

Is an image match a proof?

No. The match is experimental and ranks candidates by visual distance. It never validates a conclusion: you examine the details and decide the verdict.

How do I save a page or an image from Safari?

Open the page, tap Share and choose Portulan (turn it on under More if needed). The selection is placed in a local inbox: open Portulan › From Safari, then check the publication date, the licence, the caption and the state of the image.

How do investigations reach my other devices?

Through your own iCloud Drive, with the same Apple Account on each device. Open the app on the iPhone after a validation on the Mac to relay the sheet to the Apple Watch.

Can I protect an investigation?

Yes. A protected investigation asks for Face ID or your passcode, appears under a neutral label in the list and is left out of cross-searches. You can also export an investigation encrypted with a passphrase.

Is the app accessible?

The app uses standard system controls and text styles. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and the atlas and the Pro workshop are displayed in web views where some elements may need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
