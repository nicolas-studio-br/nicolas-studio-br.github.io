Verify before you conclude.

A native workspace for open-source investigation: compare images, cite documents, watch sources and write a conclusion that separates what is shown from what is unknown.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch

Compare two images

Look for matching details between images with Apple’s Vision similarity. The match is experimental and never validates a conclusion: you examine the enlargements and choose the verdict.

Cite your documents

Import PDFs (up to 400 pages), search them, select an exact passage and save the citation with its page. A number keeps its source before you add anything up.

Dates and origins

Record publication, page capture, file archiving and the supporting reference separately. These dates are declared by you: an archive link alone does not certify that a file was archived.

Offline atlas

Overlay maps and imagery (GeoJSON, georeferenced PNG or JPEG, .portmap), align them on landmarks, draw an area and a period, and save the view in the investigation.

Source watch

Add an HTTPS source with its licence, choose an interval and see what changed, by date. Text, text HTML, CSV and JSON up to 2 MiB.

From Safari and the field

Share a page or an image from Safari, capture a field photo with its position and tilt, and carry up to twenty recent sheets on your Apple Watch.

Publish and export

A one-page A4 sheet or a full edition as PDF, a ZIP with the evidence, and a reopenable .portulan file. A correction can cite the SHA-256 of the previous PDF.

Pro workshop

Map tile previews (MBTiles, GeoPackage), web archive (WACZ) and spreadsheet readers, signed reviews, RFC 3161 timestamp requests and connectors to public archives.

Private by design

No account, no analytics. Investigations stay on your devices and move through your own iCloud Drive. Protected investigations ask for Face ID or your passcode; exports can be encrypted with a passphrase.

Please note. Portulan helps you organise and document evidence. Similarity scores, summaries, translations and transcriptions are aids that can be wrong, and they never replace your own verification. It is not a legal, forensic or journalistic certification. The interface is available in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later, a Mac with macOS 15 or later, and for the companion app an Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. Summaries use Apple Intelligence where the device and system (version 26 or later) provide it.
