Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

L’Avocat du Dimanche does not sell or share personal data, and the developer operates no server and receives none of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. In local play, everything stays on your device. In remote play, the first names, pleas and verdicts of a room are stored in iCloud and are visible only to the players you invite (see “Remote play” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your history of cases (“family case law”): the case, players’ first names, pleas and verdict, kept in a local file.
  • Your settings: language, game mode, judge temperament, family mode and sound.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications: used for remote play, to tell you it is your turn to plead, that a verdict has been delivered or that a player joined your room. They are shown on your device.
  • Apple Intelligence and translation: verdicts and questions are generated on your device when Apple Intelligence is available, and a text in another language is translated on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Voice: the verdict is read aloud with your device’s speech synthesis. The app does not record your voice.
  • Age range: on iPhone and iPad, the app may ask Apple whether you are over 18 to decide whether family mode can be turned off. The app only receives the answer from Apple; on Mac it asks you to confirm that you are an adult.
  • Face ID or device passcode: used only to confirm that the device owner is present before family mode is turned off. The app never receives your biometric data. For a room you host, local reminders can tell you when players have arrived and when you can start; they are created and shown on your device.

Remote play (iCloud)

Remote play is optional. A room is created in the host’s private iCloud database and shared, through Apple’s sharing feature, only with the players the host invites. The room contains the players’ first names, their pleas and the verdict, and every invited player can read and write in it.

Data is stored in iCloud with the Apple Accounts of the players and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. Do not write sensitive personal information in a plea: it is shared with the other players of the room. You can leave a room, or turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS settings.

Each player’s device also publishes, in the room, whether family mode is on, so that the strictest setting applies to everyone in the room. This is a yes/no value, not an age.

Sharing

The app shares nothing with us or with third parties. Within remote play, what you write is visible to the other players of your room, as described above.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the app (you can delete cases from the history) or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Room data can be removed by the host by deleting the room, or from your iCloud storage settings. Backups made by iOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

The app is designed for families, with a family-mode content filter that is on by default and cannot be turned off by accounts that Apple reports as under 18. It does not collect data from anyone. A parent can use Apple’s parental controls to manage iCloud and notifications for a child.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.
