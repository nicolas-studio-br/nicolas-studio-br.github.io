Settle absurd debates, with full ceremony.

A playful courtroom game for family and friends: everyone pleads their case, then the Court delivers a theatrical verdict that can be read aloud.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch

Pass-the-phone mode

2 to 8 players on a single device: each one pleads, then the Court rules. Nothing else needed.

The Court’s verdict

A verdict that uses the players’ first names, written on the device with Apple Intelligence where available, with ready-made templates as a fallback.

Adjustable bad faith

Choose the judge’s temperament, from “Masterful” (rigorous) to “Totally biased” (pure whim).

Endless cases

Questions generated for an endless game, with a bank of 200 family-friendly questions per language as a fallback.

Family mode

A content filter in French and Portuguese, on by default. In a remote room the strictest setting applies to everyone. Turning it off requires Apple’s age signal (iPhone, iPad) or your device passcode or Face ID, then an “I am an adult” confirmation.

Theatrical voice and French / Brazilian Portuguese

The verdict can be read aloud by a dramatic voice: choose a male or female voice among those installed on your device (no voice is supplied by the app), and switch the app language on the fly.

Remote play, in testing

Create a room and invite up to 8 players through iCloud, with no server of ours. You can host or join several rooms at once. Still being tested before release. After a verdict an appeal can be requested; an Apple Watch companion to follow and plead is being tested too.

Please note. It is a game for fun: verdicts are humorous, not legal advice. Some features, such as remote play, are still being tested before release.

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later, or a Mac with macOS 26 or later. The Apple Watch companion (testing) requires watchOS 26 or later. The app interface is in French and Brazilian Portuguese.
