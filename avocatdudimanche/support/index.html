Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

How many players can play?

From 2 to 8 players per game, in local play and in a remote room.

Which languages does the app speak?

French and Brazilian Portuguese. Players of the two languages can play together, with translation done on each device.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. If it is not available on your device, the app uses ready-made templates and its question bank.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes: family mode is on by default. A parent can also use Apple’s parental controls.

Can family mode be turned off by anyone?

No. If Apple reports an account as under 18, family mode stays on. Otherwise the device passcode or Face ID must be verified, then you confirm that you are an adult. This checks that you know the device code; it cannot prove your age.

Is the app accessible?

The app is built with standard system controls and text styles, which follow your Dynamic Type size and work with VoiceOver. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and some custom elements may still need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
