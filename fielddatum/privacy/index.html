Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

FieldDatum does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. If you use the iCloud features, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your projects: survey coordinates, instrument imports, nominal layouts, observations, structure inspections and the photos or measurements you add.
  • Your settings.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: used for indicative AR measurements and bolt detection. Images are processed on your device.
  • Photos: you choose the photos to import with Apple’s photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select, not your whole library.
  • Location (while using the app): used for the survey position when you enable it. It is not uploaded.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

iCloud

FieldDatum can save private snapshots of your project archive to your iCloud database and a manual backup to iCloud Drive. There is no automatic merge between devices.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Sharing

FieldDatum shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

FieldDatum is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
