Survey QA and CSV import

Import instrument coordinates from CSV and check them against nominal layouts.

Coordinates

Convert WGS84 to ECEF, ENU and a local grid, with geometry tools for quick calculations.

AR measurements

Indicative AR distance, column verticality, anchor check and bolt groups of 4 to 24 bolts.

Projects and reports

Organise observations by project and export JSON data or a PDF report, including structure inspections from survey coordinates.

iCloud backup

Private iCloud snapshots of your project archive and a manual iCloud Drive backup.

Please note. AR measurements are indicative. They are not suitable for dimensional acceptance without independent qualification.
