Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Promptarium does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. If you use the iCloud features, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your prompts (title, text, notes, keywords), your lists of AIs and types, and your settings (language, favourites, usage counters).
  • To apply the 3-day trial across your devices and after a reinstall, the app stores the trial start date on your device and in iCloud key-value storage: a date and a random device identifier, with no personal information.
  • If you choose “Send feedback”, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message (app version, system version, language, access status). Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Clipboard: the app writes to it when you copy a prompt. It reads it only when you tap “Paste” or “New from clipboard”.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your prompts and lists are synchronised between your devices through Apple’s CloudKit, in your private database. It is tied to your Apple Account and is not accessible to the developer. Deleting a prompt removes it from all your devices through iCloud.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your purchase is active.

Sharing

Promptarium shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

Promptarium is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
