Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does Promptarium collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Where are my prompts stored?

On your devices and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The developer cannot access them.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You can still read, search, copy and delete prompts. Creating, editing or reorganising opens the purchase screen.

Does deleting a prompt delete it everywhere?

Yes: with iCloud on, deletion applies on all your devices.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also restore purchases from the app’s settings.
