Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

Vinarium does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. If iCloud is available, your cellar can be synced through your own iCloud account, which we cannot access (see “iCloud” below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your cellar: bottles (name, appellation, estate, vintage, grape varieties, type, tasting window, location, quantity, low-stock threshold, notes and photo), racks and the appellations and grape varieties you add.
  • Your settings: appearance, default quantity and cellar display mode.
  • Trial start date, also kept in iCloud key-value storage (when available) so that reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: used to photograph bottle labels and to show your racks in augmented reality. Label text is read on the device (Apple Vision framework); images are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos: if you choose a picture from your library, the app receives only the picture you select.
  • Notifications: used only for local low-stock alerts created on your device. No push service operated by us is involved.
  • Purchases: the one-time purchase is processed by Apple (App Store, StoreKit 2). We receive no payment details. On Mac there is no camera access: you choose an image file instead.

iCloud

iCloud sync is optional and uses your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and iCloud key-value storage, with your own Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app works with local storage only.

Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS settings.

The trial start date is also written to iCloud key-value storage, when iCloud is available, so that a reinstall does not restart the trial.

Sharing and backups

Vinarium shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a backup, you decide where it goes. Importing a backup merges or replaces your cellar as you choose.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Data synced with iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings. Backups made by iOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

Vinarium is about wine and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
