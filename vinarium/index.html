Your wine cellar, organised.

Catalogue your bottles, read labels on the device, locate every rack in augmented reality and get food-pairing ideas.

iPhone, iPad, Mac

Your cellar, by rack

Define your racks by columns and rows, place bottles in any rack (they need not be adjacent) and move them with your finger.

Label scan

Photograph a label: the text is read on the device and a bottle record is prefilled for you to check.

Pairing ideas

Rule-based suggestions of the wine type to serve with a dish, based on the tasting window you set.

Cellar in AR

Place the grid of a rack at real scale on a wall or a cabinet and highlight every bottle of a reference.

Low-stock alerts

Set a threshold on each bottle and receive a local notification when the quantity drops below it.

Backup and iCloud

Optional iCloud sync of your cellar and settings, plus a JSON backup you can export and restore.

3-day trial, then one-time purchase

Every feature is free for 3 days. After that your cellar stays readable; adding and editing need the one-time “Vinarium complet” purchase (0.99 € in France; the price is shown in the App Store).

Please note. Pairings are general suggestions, not an expert opinion. AR placement depends on your device’s sensors. Please enjoy wine responsibly.

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later, or a Mac with macOS 15 or later. The camera and AR need an iPhone or iPad (on Mac you import an image instead). The app interface is in French only.
