Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does Vinarium collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is the app available in English?

Not yet: the interface is in French only.

Is the label scan sent to a server?

No. The text is read on your device.

Is iCloud sync required?

No. Your cellar is stored on the device; iCloud sync and the JSON backup are optional.

How do I place a rack in augmented reality?

Open the AR tab, choose a rack, aim at the wall or cabinet and tap. You can adjust the spacing between slots in the settings.

Is Vinarium free?

All features are free for 3 days. After that you can keep viewing your cellar, and a one-time purchase (“Vinarium complet”, 0.99 € in France) unlocks adding and editing again. The price shown in the App Store applies.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes: the same app runs on Mac (macOS 15 or later). The AR tab is not available on Mac; the rack view and image import replace it.

Is the app accessible?

The app is built with standard system controls and text styles, which follow your Dynamic Type size and work with VoiceOver. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and some custom elements may still need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
