Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does WristWind Lab collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is this an anemometer?

No. It is an experiment. Compare it with a reference anemometer before trusting any figure, and never use it for safety.

Does the app record sound?

No. The microphone is analysed for low-frequency aerodynamic noise; audio is neither recorded nor transmitted.

How do I get a more reliable reading?

Stay still, measure the floor in calm air, then calibrate with at least two different wind speeds, ideally three or more, against a reference anemometer.

Is the app accessible?

The app is built with standard system controls and text styles, which follow your Dynamic Type size and work with VoiceOver. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and some custom elements may still need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
