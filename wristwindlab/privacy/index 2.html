Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

WristWind Lab has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK, and the developer operates no server. Audio is analysed on the watch and is neither recorded nor transmitted. The only network request is for local weather from Apple Weather (see below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings and calibration points.
  • A CSV journal of each session, made of derived values only (no audio). It is kept on the watch (the last 30 files) and transferred to your iPhone.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Microphone: low-frequency aerodynamic noise analysis. Audio is neither recorded nor transmitted.
  • Location (while using the app): approximate position for Apple Weather (air temperature, humidity, regional wind) and, if enabled, a GNSS stillness check.
  • Motion and fitness: inertial unit to check that you keep still and to follow your heading during the rotation measurement; barometer for air density and measurement height.

Apple Weather

By default the watch requests air temperature, humidity and regional wind from Apple Weather (WeatherKit) using your approximate position, at the start of a measurement and every 20 minutes. You can turn this off and type the temperature yourself. Apple’s own privacy terms apply to that request; we neither receive nor keep it. Apple Weather data attribution: https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

Sharing

WristWind Lab shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

WristWind Lab is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
