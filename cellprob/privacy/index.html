Privacy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Summary

CellProb does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and the app makes no network request. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data.

What is processed on your device

The cells, field measurements, scenario settings and results you enter are processed on your device. Your language and appearance preferences are stored locally. The app does not use iCloud.

Permissions and why they are needed

Location (while using the app): only to centre the map on your area. The position is not recorded, not sent anywhere and not added to your reports.

Reports and exports

A PDF report is generated on your device and handed to the system share sheet, where you choose the destination. The same applies to a CSV export of your cells. These files may contain coordinates, cell identifiers and times that you entered: check them before you share them.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by your system may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

CellProb is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
