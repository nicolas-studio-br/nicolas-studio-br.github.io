Cell coverage, in probabilities.

Simulate 2G to 5G radio coverage on a map and estimate, with a Monte Carlo model, which cell was most likely serving a place at a given time. Export a report with a SHA-256 fingerprint.

iPhone, iPad

Probability map

A Monte Carlo simulation (50 to 1,500 draws per point) colours the map with the chance that each cell is the strongest one. It combines COST-231 Hata path loss, the antenna pattern, indoor loss and shadow fading.

Your cell inventory

Sectors from 2G GSM 900 to 5G NR n78 (3500 MHz): CGI, position, azimuth, tilt, EIRP, traffic load and network state at the time of the event. Paste, edit or export everything as CSV.

Scenario settings

Set the date and time of the event, indoor or outdoor, one of four morphologies (dense urban, urban, suburban, rural) and the number of iterations.

Field calibration

Enter what was observed on site (serving CGI and RSRP) and compare it with the model’s prediction. The gap is shown in dB, point by point.

Ranked candidates

For the point of interest, see the candidate cells with their probability and their mean RSRP, side by side.

Report with a fingerprint

Export a PDF report and get its SHA-256 fingerprint, so that anyone can check later that the file has not changed. Share it with the system share sheet.

Fast on your device

The CPU engine works everywhere; on a real iPhone or iPad you can switch on Metal GPU acceleration.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network request. Location is used only to centre the map on your area.

Your language, your look

English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil), or follow the system. Light, dark or automatic appearance.

Please note. CellProb gives probabilistic estimates from a simplified propagation model and from the data you enter. A result is not proof of where a phone was, and it does not replace operator records, a field survey or an expert opinion.

Requires an iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. The interface is available in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
