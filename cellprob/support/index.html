Support

Need help or have a suggestion? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does CellProb collect my data?

No. The app makes no network request. See the privacy policy for the details.

What does a probability mean?

It is the share of simulated draws in which that cell had the strongest signal at the point, above the model’s minimum level. It describes the model and the data you entered, not a certainty.

Is a result proof?

No. It is an estimate. Field calibration, the quality of the cell data and the choice of morphology all change the result. Verify important findings independently.

Where do the cell data come from?

CellProb does not download operator data. You enter your own cells or paste a CSV (site, CGI, technology, position, azimuth, tilt, EIRP). Export a CSV from the app to see the expected columns.

How do I check that a report has not been modified?

Each exported PDF comes with its SHA-256 fingerprint. Compute the fingerprint of the file you received and compare it with the one in the app or the one you were given.

Is the app accessible?

The app uses standard system controls and text styles. It has not been audited by an independent third party. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
