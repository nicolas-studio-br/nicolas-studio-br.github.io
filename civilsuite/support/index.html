Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does CivilSuite collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Calculations work offline. Internet is used for the site weather and place names (Apple services) and for iCloud if you turn it on.

Are GPS coordinates included when I share a file?

Not by default: the “Remove GPS coordinates” option is on. Photos are re-encoded and keep only the capture date and orientation. Check the content before sending.

The app does not use my photo library. Why?

It uses Apple’s photo picker, which passes on only the photos you select. The app never asks for access to your whole library.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You can also restore purchases from the app’s settings.
