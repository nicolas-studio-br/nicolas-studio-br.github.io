Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

CivilSuite has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer operates no server. Your calculations, reports and photos stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn it on. Weather and place names come from Apple services (see below).

What is stored on your device

  • Your calculations, records, site reports and photos. Crack photos are analysed on the device and are not sent to anyone.
  • A .civilsuite file you export contains the calculations and reports you selected, their photos, the author you declare (name, company), the export date and the history of previous authors. The “Remove GPS coordinates” option, on by default, removes site and photo locations. Photos are re-encoded and keep only the capture date and orientation. Check the content before sending.
  • Your settings and your purchase status, provided by Apple.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: to photograph a crack or illustrate a site report.
  • Location (only while the app is open): to get the weather and the place name for a report and, if you choose, to record where report photos were taken.
  • Photos: you choose the photos to import with Apple’s photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select, not your whole library.
  • iCloud: see the “iCloud” section below.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud backup, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple’s frameworks. To apply a deletion on all your devices, the app also stores a short deletion record there (identifier and date, no content). If you turn on instant sync, calculations and records are exchanged between your devices through the app’s private CloudKit database; to be told about a change, the app receives silent notifications from Apple and asks for no notification permission. The Apple Watch app receives the last weather and the pour progress from the iPhone and sends back the truck arrivals you record; nothing leaves your devices. The Live Activity and Siri shortcuts run on the device; requests made to Siri are handled by Apple under its own policy.

These features use Apple’s iCloud service with your own Apple Account. Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in the iOS or macOS settings.

Apple services: weather and maps

For the weather, the app sends the site location and the requested period to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). To find a place name, its time zone or an address you type, it uses Apple Maps geocoding (MapKit). These requests are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we neither receive nor keep this information. Apple Weather data attribution: https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether your purchase is active.

Sharing

CivilSuite shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

CivilSuite is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
