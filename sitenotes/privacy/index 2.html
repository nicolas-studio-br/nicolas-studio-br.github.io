Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

SiteReport does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data.

What is stored on your device

  • Your notes: title, content, project, zone, category, status, responsible person, due date and, if you record it, a position.
  • Your photos. Photos imported from your library keep their original metadata (including GPS position) inside the app and in backups. PDF reports never contain this metadata.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, coordinate format and your list of projects and zones.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera: to take photos that you attach to your notes. Photos are stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. The Mac version does not use the camera.
  • Photos: you choose the photos to import with Apple’s photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select, not your whole library.
  • Location (while using the app): read only when you tap “Record current position”. It is saved in the note you choose and is never sent anywhere.
  • Map: the Map tab shows Apple Maps. To draw the map, your device downloads map data from Apple, which receives the area displayed, under Apple’s privacy policy. Your notes and their positions are not sent.
  • Dictation and Siri: if you dictate text or use Siri, your speech is processed by Apple according to your device settings.
  • Files: you choose which files the app opens. The app reads only the files you select.

Sharing

SiteReport shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export a file, a report or a backup, you decide where it goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Backups made by iOS or macOS may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

SiteReport is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Contact: nicolas_studio@proton.me.
