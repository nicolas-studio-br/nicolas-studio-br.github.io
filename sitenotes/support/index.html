Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does SiteReport collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), inside the app: the app sends none of your data. Only the Map tab downloads map backgrounds from Apple Maps. Use “Export a backup” in Settings to keep a copy.

Is the backup file encrypted?

No. The backup holds all notes and photos with their metadata, so keep it somewhere safe. Its size limit is 100 MB.

Do PDF reports contain my GPS data?

Not by default. Coordinates are excluded unless you choose to include them, and photos are re-encoded without metadata.

The map background does not appear.

Base maps may need an internet connection. Your note positions themselves are stored on your device.

Does SiteReport work on iPad and Mac?

Yes. SiteReport runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS/iPadOS 18 or later) and natively on Mac (macOS 15 or later). On Mac there is no camera: add photos from the Photos library.

Is the app accessible?

The app is built with standard system controls and text styles, which follow your Dynamic Type size and work with VoiceOver. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and some custom elements may still need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
