Support

Need help? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does SiteNotes collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your iPhone: the app sends nothing over the network. Use “Export a backup” in Settings to keep a copy.

Is the backup file encrypted?

No. The backup holds all notes and photos with their metadata, so keep it somewhere safe. Its size limit is 100 MB.

Do PDF reports contain my GPS data?

Not by default. Coordinates are excluded unless you choose to include them, and photos are re-encoded without metadata.

The map background does not appear.

Base maps may need an internet connection. Your note positions themselves are stored on the iPhone.
