Site notes, with photos and position.

Write down what you see on site: photos, position, status and who owns each action. Everything stays on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone · iPad · Mac

Structured notes

Project, zone, category, location on site, action, status (to do, in progress, closed), person responsible and due date.

Photos

Add up to 8 photos per note, from the camera or the photo picker.

Position and map

Your position is recorded only when you tap the button. Located notes appear on a map.

PDF reports

Filter your notes and create a PDF report. Photos are re-encoded without metadata and GPS coordinates are left out by default.

Backup and Shortcuts

Export a full backup file and restore it later. Open the app or draft a note by voice with Shortcuts.

Faster on site

Projects and zones are suggested as you type, favourites first. Duplicate a note to record a similar observation in seconds.

Coordinates your way

Show positions in WGS 84 decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds or UTM, in the app and in PDF reports.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

The same app runs on iPhone, iPad and natively on Mac, with keyboard shortcuts on iPad and Mac.

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 18 or later, or a Mac with macOS 15 or later. Interface in English, Français, Español and Português (Brasil).
