Support

Need help or have a suggestion? Write to nicolas_studio@proton.me (in the app: Settings › Contact and suggestions) and include the app name and version, your device and system version, and what happened. Start with the answers below.

Does LinkShelf collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy for the details.

Are my summaries sent to an AI service?

No. They are generated on your device by Apple’s model, and only on devices where Apple Intelligence is available.

How do I save a link from Safari?

Use the share button and choose LinkShelf. The link is added the next time the app opens.

What does Pro include?

Offline reading, highlights and a link checker, as a one-time purchase. Everything else is free.

Is iCloud sync required?

No. Your library is stored on the device; iCloud sync and exports are optional.

Is the app accessible?

The app is built with standard system controls and text styles, which follow your Dynamic Type size and work with VoiceOver. It has not been audited by an independent third party, and some custom elements may still need improvement. If you meet a barrier, write to nicolas_studio@proton.me and describe it: it will be fixed as a priority.
