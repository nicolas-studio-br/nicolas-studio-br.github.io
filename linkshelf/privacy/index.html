Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

LinkShelf does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. The developer operates no server and receives none of your data. When you save a link, the app contacts that page directly to read its title and text; your library is synced only through your own iCloud.

What is stored on your device

  • Your library: saved links (address, title, site name, description, content type, summary, notes, tags, folder, favourite and read status, the beginning of the page text used for search), files you add (25 MB max each), highlights and offline copies (Pro).
  • Your settings: language, appearance and options.
  • Links handed over by the share extension are placed in a shared container (App Group) on your device until the app imports them.
  • Items you delete go to a trash for 30 days, then are permanently removed.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Network: when you save a link, the app requests that page directly to read its title, description, type and readable text. The site you saved sees the request, like any browser visit (including your IP address). With Pro, the link checker contacts your saved links in the same way. No third-party service is involved.
  • Files: on Mac, you choose which files to open; the app reads only those.
  • Notifications: only for local “read later” reminders that you create. No push service operated by us is involved.
  • Apple Intelligence: summaries are generated on your device with Apple’s Foundation Models framework, when available. Nothing leaves your device for this.
  • Purchases: the one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple (App Store, StoreKit 2). We receive no payment details.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: the app can add a link through App Intents, handled by the system.

iCloud

iCloud sync is optional and uses your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and iCloud key-value storage, with your own Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app works with local storage only.

Your data is stored in your private iCloud space and protected by Apple; the developer cannot see it and has no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn iCloud off for this app in your system settings.

Sharing and exports

LinkShelf shares nothing with us or with third parties. If you export your library (JSON, Markdown, CSV or HTML) you decide where the file goes.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app, which removes the app’s local data. Data synced with iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings. Backups made by your system may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children

LinkShelf is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question under the GDPR, the LGPD or any other privacy law, write to us.

Changes and contact
