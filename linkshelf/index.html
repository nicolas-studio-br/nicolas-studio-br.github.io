Your links and files, on one shelf.

Save web links and files, let on-device AI summarise them, sort them into folders and tags, and find them again. Synced through your own iCloud.

Not yet available on the App Store Ask about availability
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision

Save anything

Paste a link, tap +, drag and drop, share from Safari or any app, add a file (25 MB max) or use a Siri Shortcut.

Knows what it saved

The app recognises 19 kinds of content (article, video, recipe, place…) from the domain, schema.org data, Open Graph and the file extension.

Private summaries

Summaries are written by Apple’s on-device model (Foundation Models) when Apple Intelligence is available. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

Organise

Folders and tags with colours, pinned items, favourites, read/unread, a notepad for your own notes, a 30-day trash and optional “read later” reminders.

Find it again

Search titles, notes and the beginning of the page text; browse by calendar; see statistics on what you save.

Yours to take away

iCloud sync, plus export to JSON, Markdown, CSV and HTML (favourites) and JSON import. Tracking parameters are removed from saved links.

Free, with a one-time Pro

The library is free. A one-time Pro purchase adds offline reading, highlights and a link checker.

Please note. Summaries and content types are automatic and can be wrong. Saved pages can change or disappear; you are responsible for the rights to content you save. The interface is in English, Français, Português (Brasil) and Español, switchable in the app.

Requires iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later, a Mac with macOS 26 or later, or Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 26 or later. Summaries need a device with Apple Intelligence.
